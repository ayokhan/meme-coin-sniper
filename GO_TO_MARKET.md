# NovaStaris — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Product:** NovaStaris (novastaris.ai) — Advanced AI Lightning Sniper for meme coins and wallet-led signals  
**Last updated:** 2025

---

## 1. Executive summary

NovaStaris targets retail crypto traders and wallet followers who want to discover meme coins early, follow “smart money” wallets, and use AI to evaluate tokens on Solana and BSC. This document outlines target market, positioning, pricing, channels, and launch tactics to grow awareness, sign-ups, and paid conversions (Pro/VIP).

---

## 2. Target market

### 2.1 Primary

- **Retail meme coin traders** (Solana and BSC): Active in degens, new launches, and trending tokens; comfortable with wallets and basic DeFi.
- **Wallet followers:** Users who track specific wallets (CT influencers, “smart money”) and want alerts when those wallets buy the same token.
- **Geographic:** Global; English-first. Note regional restrictions for futures (e.g. Ontario: use Blofin; KuCoin/Hyperliquid restrictions as documented).

### 2.2 Secondary

- **Crypto futures traders** interested in signals or automated strategies (Futures tab, optional trading bot).
- **Communities:** Telegram/Discord groups focused on Solana or BSC meme coins; CT (Crypto Twitter) audiences.

### 2.3 TAM / SAM / SOM (illustrative)

- **TAM:** Retail crypto traders interested in meme coins and alpha (large, growing).
- **SAM:** Traders who use dashboards, trackers, or AI tools for token discovery and signals.
- **SOM:** Users who discover NovaStaris via CT, Telegram, SEO, or referrals and convert to free or paid.

---

## 3. Positioning

### 3.1 Value proposition

- **For discovery:** “Find new and trending meme coins on Solana and BSC in one place—new pairs, final stretch, migrated, trending.”
- **For signals:** “Get alerts when multiple tracked wallets buy the same token; never miss coordinated moves.”
- **For AI:** “NovaStaris AI gives you a viral score and clear buy/no-buy signal so you can decide faster.”

### 3.2 Differentiation

- **Combined stack:** New pairs + trending + surge + Wallet Tracker + AI analysis + (Pro/VIP) BSC AI and Coach Calls.
- **Wallet-led alerts:** First-buy and multi-wallet alerts with Telegram delivery.
- **Owner-led support:** Live chat and support tickets for paying users; transparent Status page for integrations (owner-only).

### 3.3 Messaging pillars

1. **Speed:** “Meme coins move fast—we surface new pairs and trending tokens in one dashboard.”
2. **Smart money:** “Track wallets you trust; get alerted when they buy the same token.”
3. **AI edge:** “Viral score and recommendations so you can act with confidence.”

---

## 4. Pricing strategy

### 4.1 Current model

- **Free:** Full Solana discovery (new pairs, trending, surge, CT Scan, Wallet Tracker view, Solana AI Analysis, Crypto Futures, BSC discovery tabs). Pins and core features for signed-in users.
- **Pro:** Paid tier; includes BSC AI Analysis and other Pro benefits; Solana USDC subscription.
- **VIP:** Higher tier; Coach Calls, Telegram signals, premium support; Solana USDC.

### 4.2 Pricing tactics

- **Clear upgrade path:** In-app prompts (e.g. “Upgrade to Pro” for BSC AI); subscribe page with plan comparison.
- **Retention:** Subscription expiry and renewal flows; optional email (e.g. Resend) for payment and product updates.
- **Positioning:** Pro/VIP as “edge” and “signals” rather than generic “premium.”

---

## 5. Channels & distribution

### 5.1 Owned

- **Website (novastaris.ai):** Primary product; SEO-optimized pages (About, features, pricing/subscribe).
- **Blog/Content (if added):** How-to’s (e.g. “How to use Wallet Tracker,” “How to read AI score”) for search and trust.
- **Email (if added):** Onboarding, reset password, subscription and product updates (Resend).

### 5.2 Earned

- **Crypto Twitter (CT):** Engage with meme coin and alpha communities; share product updates, tips, and Status (when appropriate); encourage word-of-mouth.
- **Telegram / Discord:** Participate in relevant groups; offer NovaStaris as a tool for discovery and alerts; VIP/Coach Calls can drive referrals.
- **Reviews and comparisons:** Encourage users to compare NovaStaris to other snipers/trackers; address “vs” searches with clear differentiation.

### 5.3 Paid (optional)

- **CT / Telegram ads:** Targeted campaigns to meme coin and Solana/BSC audiences.
- **Influencer / affiliate:** Partner with CT or Telegram influencers for reviews or promo codes (track via UTM or referral if implemented).

### 5.4 Partnerships

- **Data/API:** Already integrated with DexScreener, Moralis, Birdeye, etc.; no formal partnership required for current use.
- **Communities:** Align with Solana/BSC communities and events (hackathons, Twitter Spaces) for visibility.

## 6. Launch & growth phases

### 6.1 Phase 1 — Launch / awareness

- **Goal:** Establish presence and first users.
- **Actions:** Soft launch to CT and Telegram; share Status and key features; collect feedback; fix critical issues; document everything (TECH_STACK_AND_APIS, PRD, GTM, JTBD).
- **Success:** First 50–100 sign-ups; first Pro/VIP conversions; stable uptime (Status green).

### 6.2 Phase 2 — Growth

- **Goal:** Scale sign-ups and conversions.
- **Actions:** Double down on channels that drive sign-ups (CT, Telegram, SEO); add onboarding or email (e.g. Resend) if needed; refine pricing and upgrade prompts; consider affiliate or promo codes.
- **Success:** Steady MRR growth; lower cost per acquisition; higher retention.

### 6.3 Phase 3 — Retention & expansion

- **Goal:** Retain and expand revenue per user.
- **Actions:** Coach Calls and VIP benefits; better support (chat, tickets); optional new features (e.g. more chains, more alerts); re-engagement for lapsed subscribers.
- **Success:** Higher LTV; referrals; reduced churn.

## 8. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| API dependency (DexScreener, Moralis, etc.) | Monitor Status; document fallbacks; consider multiple data sources where already implemented. |
| Regional restrictions (futures, exchanges) | Document in product and GTM (e.g. Ontario → Blofin); avoid marketing futures in restricted regions. |
| Low conversion free → paid | Clarify Pro/VIP value (BSC AI, Coach Calls); A/B test upgrade prompts and pricing. |
| Churn after first month | Onboarding, email, and support; Coach Calls and signals as retention drivers. |
