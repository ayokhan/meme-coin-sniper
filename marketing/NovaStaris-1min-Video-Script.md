## Production notes

- **Style:** Clean, modern, dark or light theme to match novastaris.ai. Cyan/violet/blue gradient for logo and key text.
- **Music:** Short, upbeat, non-intrusive (no lyrics) — build slightly to the final CTA.
- **Voice:** Confident, clear, slightly faster than normal to keep energy; or use text-on-screen only for silent autoplay (Instagram/TikTok).
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/TikTok; 1:1 or 16:9 for YouTube/Landing page.
- **Tools you can use:** Canva Video, Loom (screen + voice), Descript, Runway, or a freelancer on Fiverr/Upwork with this script + shot list.
