# Terms of Service & Privacy — Liability and Compliance

## Do you need them?

**Yes.** For an app that:

- Collects email and personal data (registration, newsletter)
- Provides trading-related information (signals, analytics, perp digest)
- Operates in crypto/financial context

having **Terms of Service** and a **Privacy Policy** is standard and helps with:

1. **Liability** — Clear disclaimers (no investment advice, no guarantee of accuracy, use at your own risk) help limit liability if users suffer losses.
2. **Privacy compliance** — Many regions (e.g. GDPR, CCPA) expect a public privacy policy explaining what you collect, why, and how users can exercise rights.
3. **User agreement** — By registering or using the app, users agree to your terms, which can include acceptable use and limitation of liability.

## What’s in this repo

- **`/terms`** — Terms of Service page (placeholder/summary). States: informational use only, no advice, no liability, no guarantee, use at your own risk.
- **`/privacy`** — Privacy Policy page (placeholder/summary). States: what you collect, how you use it, newsletter opt-in, no selling data, option to have a lawyer draft full policy.
- **Register page** — Optional line: “By creating an account you agree to our [Terms](/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/privacy).”

## Recommended next steps

1. **Have a lawyer draft full documents** tailored to your entity, jurisdiction, and exact features. The in-app pages are summaries and explicitly say a lawyer should draft the full Terms and Privacy Policy.
2. **Keep them updated** when you add features (e.g. payments, more data collection, new regions).
3. **Link from sign-in/register** so users see the agreement before or at registration.
4. If you operate in the EU or California, ensure your Privacy Policy covers GDPR/CCPA (rights, legal basis, retention, etc.); a lawyer can do this properly.

You are not required to have a lawyer to publish *something*, but for real liability and compliance protection, professional legal review is recommended.
